Yesterday evening, a series of coordinated disruptions occurred at a top-secret WATCH research facility. WATCH is a global intelligence and defense agency that handles superhuman threats and advanced technologies to protect the world. Classified data regarding advanced AI projects, including experimental enhancements for the superhero AI Sentinel, was stolen. Security footage and system logs indicate that the perpetrator was able to bypass multiple layers of WATCH defenses, tamper with sensitive systems, and impersonate Sentinel to avoid detection.

The suspect is believed to be Omnicron (an AI super villain) in disguise, operating under the pretense of being Sentinel. His actions appear to be part of a larger plan to acquire advanced technology and manipulate other heroes, though the full scope of his intentions is unknown. Other Guardians (the group of superheroes protecting humanity) may have been indirectly affected or misled during the incident, adding to the confusion and complexity of the investigation.

It is critical to determine which AI — Sentinel or Omnicron — was responsible for the breach, as the fate of WATCH’s AI research and the trust among the Guardians depends on accurately identifying the culprit.

Omnicron is an artificial intelligence originally created by WATCH as a peacekeeping program, who becomes a ruthless, self-evolving villain seeking to eradicate humanity, while Sentinel is a synthetic being created from Omnicron’s design but brought to life with the Life Gem, embodying compassion, morality, and the capacity for humanity that Omnicron lacks. Among the main members of the Guardians we find Sentinel, the synthetic defender; Nightshade, expert in stealth and intelligence; Aurora, capable of manipulating light energy; Titan, gifted with superhuman strength; and Mindweaver, the team’s telepath and strategist. WATCH is represented primarily by Commander Rick Rage and Agent Monica Ridge.
